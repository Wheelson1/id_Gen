WHY LEGACY CAMPUS IDs FAIL

The Problem vs. The SID Platform

Traditional PVC cards with static barcodes offer zero protection against colour photocopying, exam impersonation, or unverified fee collection.

LEGACY UNIVERSITY ID WORKFLOW

Vulnerable, Manual & Unverifiable

  • 30-Second Photocopy Forgery: Static plastic cards and unencrypted QR codes can be duplicated at any business centre.
  • Exam Hall Impersonation: Invigilators cannot verify whether a card has been revoked, suspended, or altered after printing.
  • Weeks of Physical Queues: Students queue at ICT centres to submit paper receipts and physical passport photos.
  • Revenue Leakage: Manual bank-teller verification makes reconciling per-student ID card fees slow and error-prone.
EBSU SID CRYPTOGRAPHIC PLATFORM

Verifiable, Self-Service & Instant

  • Asymmetric Ed25519 Signatures: Every card is signed over card_id + matric + name + expiry. Any database name edit automatically invalidates stale signatures.
  • 60-Second Rotating Gate Pass (EBSU2): Students generate a live 60-second single-use pass on their phone for exam entry; re-scanning a screenshot triggers an instant REPLAYED alert.
  • 60-Second Self-Service Onboarding: Students verify JAMB + DOB, pay the ₦2,500 card fee, and frame their passport photo online.
  • Zero Hardware Lock-In: Campus security guards verify cards using any smartphone camera via verify.html.
DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH

Four-Layer Security Architecture

Engineered to withstand enumeration attacks, credential replay, client-side privilege escalation, and unauthorized card issuance.

01 · ANTI-ENUMERATION

Dual-Bucket DOB Lockout + Turnstile

Student lookup requires JAMB + Date of Birth + Cloudflare Turnstile (failing closed if unconfigured) and enforces lockouts both per (jamb_no, ip) and globally per jamb_no across rotating IPs.

02 · PRE-PAYMENT PRIVACY

Zero Pre-Payment PII Leakage

Before the ₦2,500 card fee is verified, the server withholds the student's matriculation number, official portrait URL, and QR signature from both the DOM and network responses.

03 · STAFF AAL2 ENCLAVE

Hardware-Enforced TOTP MFA

Registrar, Admin, and Super Admin actions require aal2 Multi-Factor Authentication enforced directly inside PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (has_required_mfa()) and Edge Functions.

04 · IMMUTABLE AUDIT

Tamper-Evident Audit & Scan Logs

Every card issuance, revocation, manual registration approval, and gate scan verdict (valid, replayed, forged) is recorded with actor and IP metadata.
