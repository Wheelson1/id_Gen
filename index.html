Help and how it works

How it works

Four steps, all on this page. You can stop after paying and come back later with your payment reference.

  1. Find your record

    Enter your JAMB or matric number. We look for your admission record in the university register.

  2. Confirm it is you

    You see part of the name and the department. If they match, you enter your date of birth and we check it against the record.

  3. Pay the ID card fee

    Pay on the secure payment page. The amount is shown before you pay, and you get a payment reference to keep.

  4. Get your card

    Your signed card appears straight away. Download it, print it, or show the live pass from your phone.

How a card is checked

A printed card can be photocopied. These three checks are why a copy does not pass.

Signed by the university

The QR code on every card carries a digital signature made with a key only the university holds. Change a name or a number and the signature no longer matches.

A pass that changes every minute

At gates and exam halls you show a live pass from your phone. It is replaced every 60 seconds and each one works once, so a screenshot is useless.

Checked against the register

Every scan asks the university's register for the card's status. A card reported lost or withdrawn is refused from that moment.

📸 Official Passport Photograph Standards & Framing Tool

Your passport photograph is printed directly onto your physical CR80 Smart ID Card and displayed on security scanners during gate verification. Follow these standards to avoid rejection:

✅ ACCEPTED STANDARDS

  • Plain Background: Solid white, off-white, or light sky-blue wall.
  • Head Position: Facing directly forward, both ears visible where applicable.
  • Framing: Head and top of shoulders centered, filling 70–80% of frame.
  • Lighting: Clear, even daylight on face with no harsh shadows.
  • Attire: Decent academic/formal attire or departmental dress code.

❌ AUTOMATIC REJECTION

  • No Selfies / Tilted Angles: Side profile, looking down, or arm-extended selfies.
  • No Accessories: Dark sunglasses, face masks, caps, or heavy headwraps hiding face oval.
  • No Busy Backgrounds: Hostel curtains, trees, vehicles, or other people visible.
  • No Filters: Snapchat/Instagram filters, over-bleached exposure, or blurry screenshots.
