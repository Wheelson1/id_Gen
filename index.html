ZERO-TRUST CREDENTIALING

Security by design

Ordinary plastic cards can be cloned at any street kiosk. Every EBSU credential is protected by three independent verification layers.

LAYER 01 · ASYMMETRIC CRYPTO

Ed25519 Canonical Signatures

Each card's QR payload binds the card UUID, matriculation number, full name, and expiry date using an Ed25519 private key held strictly inside the server enclave. Altering a single character invalidates the signature.

LAYER 02 · ANTI-REPLAY

60-Second Rotating Live Pass

For high-security exam halls and hostel gates, students mint a dynamic EBSU2 pass that expires in 60 seconds and is consumed on first scan—rendering screenshots and photocopies useless.

LAYER 03 · LIVE AUTHORITY

Server Verification & Instant Revocation

When a guard scans a card at verify.html, the server checks live card status, fee clearance, and displays the official stored portrait so impersonators are stopped on the spot.

📸 Official Passport Photograph Standards & Framing Tool

Your passport photograph is printed directly onto your physical CR80 Smart ID Card and displayed on security scanners during gate verification. Follow these standards to avoid rejection:

✅ ACCEPTED STANDARDS

  • Plain Background: Solid white, off-white, or light sky-blue wall.
  • Head Position: Facing directly forward, both ears visible where applicable.
  • Framing: Head and top of shoulders centered, filling 70–80% of frame.
  • Lighting: Clear, even daylight on face with no harsh shadows.
  • Attire: Decent academic/formal attire or departmental dress code.

❌ AUTOMATIC REJECTION

  • No Selfies / Tilted Angles: Side profile, looking down, or arm-extended selfies.
  • No Accessories: Dark sunglasses, face masks, caps, or heavy headwraps hiding face oval.
  • No Busy Backgrounds: Hostel curtains, trees, vehicles, or other people visible.
  • No Filters: Snapchat/Instagram filters, over-bleached exposure, or blurry screenshots.
