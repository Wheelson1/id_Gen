📐 Official Passport Photo & Signature Quality Standards

Ebonyi State University & Triple J SID Identification Card Standards

Your ID card photograph is an official biometric university credential. To ensure your card prints sharply and passes security verification, follow these criteria:

❌ Avoid (Common Mistakes)
CASUAL SELFIE
  • ❌ High/Low Angle: Looking up or down at phone camera.
  • ❌ Busy Background: Bedroom walls, fans, outdoors, or door frames.
  • ❌ Casual Attire: Sports jerseys (e.g. football kits), singlets.
  • ❌ Uneven Lighting: Harsh overhead sunlight or half-shadowed face.
  • ❌ Expression: Heavy squinting, frowning, or exaggerated poses.
✅ Recommended (Official Passport)
OFFICIAL CR80 FIT
  • ✅ Eye-Level Position: Camera held straight at eye level.
  • ✅ Solid Background: Uniform solid color (Light Blue, Red, or White).
  • ✅ Proper Framing: Head & shoulders centered, 10–15% margin at the top.
  • ✅ Formal/Smart Attire: Collared shirt, suit, blouse, or plain dark clothing.
  • ✅ Balanced Lighting: Soft, uniform front-facing light on both cheeks.

Your signature will be placed onto the security signature box of your ID card. Follow these tips so the strokes remain crisp and professional:

❌ Avoid (Hard to Process)
TEXTURED / GREY PAPER
  • ❌ Paper Shadows: Hand or phone casting dark shadows on paper.
  • ❌ Reverse Bleed: Text, newsprint, or lines showing through from back.
  • ❌ Faint Ink: Faint pencil or dried ballpoint pen with broken lines.
  • ❌ Tilted / Far: Shot from an acute angle or from far away.
✅ Recommended (Clean Isolated Ink)
Z. Somtochukwu
CLEAN / TRANSPARENT
  • ✅ Clean White Sheet: Sign on fresh, unlined blank white paper.
  • ✅ Bold Pen: Use a dark blue or black gel/rollerball pen with rich ink.
  • ✅ Direct Bird's Eye View: Shoot directly straight down from above (90°).
  • ✅ Automatic Auto-Clean: Our canvas engine isolates and deepens the ink while making the paper background transparent!

Test any photo in the interactive Passport Framing Box below. Align your face inside the green oval guideline to guarantee a clean passport fit on your ID card:

🎯 Biometric Placement Rules:
  • Eyes should align along the dashed horizontal green line.
  • Top of head/hair should sit just inside the upper curve of the oval.
  • Chin should sit cleanly in the lower curve.
