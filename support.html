Get help with your ID card

Not in the system, wrong details, a payment problem or a lost card? Choose what you need below. You don't need an account.

Or call the helpdesk: 0901 605 4526, 0903 500 5623

Ask to be added to the register

If we couldn't find your JAMB or matric number, send your details and admission letter. The Registry checks them and adds you.

Check a registration request

Use the tracking code you got when you sent your request (it starts with EBSU-REG).

Report a problem

Wrong details on your card, a payment that didn't go through, or a lost card.

Read and reply to your ticket

Use the ticket number you got when you reported the problem (it starts with EBSU-TKT).
